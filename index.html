仲間と、アニメを。

Your anime. Your friends. One app.

NakamaList is a free anime and manga tracker for Android. Sign in with MyAnimeList or AniList, keep your list up to date, and see what your friends are watching and how they rated it.

See what it does

Independent and unofficial. Not affiliated with MyAnimeList or AniList. It signs in through their official APIs with your own account.

A tracker is only half of it. The other half is who you watch with.

記録Lists

Every edit, everywhere, at once.

Track anime and manga in one list, with episodes, chapters, volumes, scores, dates, tags and notes all in the same place. Mark an episode as watched with a single tap and the change shows up on Home, in your list and on the title page at once, so you never lose track of where you are in a series. Scores can be shown out of 10, out of 100, as decimals or as five stars, and titles in romaji or in English.

仲間Friends

See what everyone is watching.

Follow friends from MyAnimeList and AniList side by side, and their updates arrive in one activity feed instead of two separate sites. Open anyone’s anime or manga list on the same screen you use for your own, and every title page shows what your friends scored it next to your score, along with their average.

発見Discover

Find the next one.

Browse the official top anime and manga rankings, see what MyAnimeList suggests for you, or search the whole MyAnimeList catalogue in full screen. Each title page has the synopsis, score, rank, characters, staff and trailer, and you can play the opening and ending themes before you decide to start watching.

季節Seasons

Never miss a new season.

See every anime airing this season, the one coming next and every season before it, each laid out as a grid of posters. Sort the season chart by score, by popularity or by start date, add shows straight to your Plan to Watch list, and get a notification when a new episode of something you are watching comes out.

話題News

The news, in the app.

Read the latest anime and manga news from MyAnimeList inside the app, with a New Anime tag for fresh announcements. The forums are there too, and every episode of a show links to its own discussion thread, so you can talk about what just happened without opening a browser.

統計Stats

Your taste in numbers.

See how many days of anime you have watched, how many chapters you have read, your mean score and how your list breaks down by status, for anime and for manga separately. Charts show the genres and formats you watch most, and achievements mark the milestones your list has reached.

画面Devices

Phone. Tablet. Television.

NakamaList works on Android phones, fills a tablet screen in landscape instead of sitting in a narrow strip, and runs on Android TV and Google TV with full remote control. It is available in 56 languages, including right-to-left ones, with white, dark and pure black themes and home-screen widgets for what you are currently watching and reading.

質問FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is NakamaList free?

Yes. NakamaList is completely free to download and use, with no ads, no subscription and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Is it an official MyAnimeList app?

No. NakamaList is an independent, unofficial client that signs in through the official MyAnimeList and AniList APIs with your own account, and it is not affiliated with either service.

Do I need a MyAnimeList account?

You can sign in with MyAnimeList, with AniList, or with both. When both are linked, every change you make on your MyAnimeList list is copied to AniList, and a Sync screen can bring the two lists in line.

Does it track manga as well as anime?

Yes. Manga, light novels and manhwa are tracked the same way as anime, with chapters, volumes, scores, dates and notes.

What Android version do I need?

NakamaList runs on Android 8.0 and newer, on phones, tablets and Android TV.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub or email nakamalist@proton.me. Bug reports and feature requests both have a template ready.
